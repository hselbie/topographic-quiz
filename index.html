🗻 Topographic Quiz

Read the contour map like a navigator. Each game mixes two kinds of question: Where am I? (find the viewpoint on the map) and Which way am I facing? (you know the point — give the compass heading of the view).

  1. 10 rounds, no time limit — a stopwatch runs while you think. Faster answers score more (max 1000 per round).
  2. Where am I? Easy/Medium: pick the right lettered point (wrong = 0). Hard: drop a pin — within 100 m = full marks, fading to zero at 600 m.
  3. Which way am I facing? The view is locked and the compass hidden. Drag the bearing arrow on the map (or type a heading) — within 5° = full marks, fading to zero at 45°. Harder tiers narrow the field of view.
  4. Named peaks (▲) are on the map. The 💡 hint labels the peaks visible in the 3D view — costs 40% of the round's points.
  5. Keyboard: A–F to answer, ←/→ nudge heading, Enter to confirm / next round.
