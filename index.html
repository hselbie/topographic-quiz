🗻 Topographic Quiz

Read the contour map like a navigator. Each round you see a topographic map with several marked points and a 3D view of the landscape from one of those points. Pick the right point as fast as you can.

  1. 10 rounds. No time limit — a stopwatch runs while you think.
  2. Correct answer: up to 1000 pts — faster is better.
  3. Wrong answer or time-out: 0 pts.
  4. Named peaks (▲) are marked on the map. Use the 💡 hint to label the peaks visible in the 3D view — it costs 40% of the round's points.
  5. Difficulty ramps up: more points, closer together.
  6. Keyboard: press A–F to answer, Enter for next round.
